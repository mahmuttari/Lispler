# Hayalet Avcıları — en-US

- **Uygulama adı:** Ghost Hunters

## Kısa açıklama

Catch ghosts, beat bosses, collect costumes! Play solo or with a friend.

## Uzun açıklama

In Ghost Hunters, ghosts are coming from everywhere! Pick your hunter, roam maze-like levels and catch the ghosts one by one.

👻 GHOST HUNT
• Move with the joystick while your hunter automatically fires at the nearest ghost.
• When the timer runs out, the level boss shows up: beat it and its little ghosts to reach the next level.
• Levels themed Purple Castle, Green Forest, Desert, Ice Cave, Volcano and Space.
• Watch out for spikes, lava and water!

🦸 CHARACTERS AND COSTUMES
• Kinkin, Veryi, Kornet, Marli, Kenit, Ponko and more: every hunter has their own special power and weapon.
• Spend the coins you collect to upgrade speed, damage, fire rate and range.
• Unlock new costumes with diamonds and emeralds.

💣 AMMO
• Bomb, Freeze, Lightning, Tornado, Rocket, Invisibility and First Aid: use them with a single tap when things get tough.

🌐 PLAY TOGETHER
• Create an online room and hunt ghosts on the same map with your friends.
• Climb to the top of the Champions list.

☁️ KEEP YOUR PROGRESS
• If you like, sign in with Google to back up your progress to the cloud and move it to another phone.

🌍 6 LANGUAGES
• Türkçe, English, Español, 简体中文, हिन्दी, العربية — the game opens in your phone's language, and you can switch it from the menu.

Free to play, no real-money purchases. In-game coins, diamonds and emeralds are earned only by playing. An ad is shown now and then when a game ends; never during play.

## Sürüm notu

Online rooms, code backups and the champions list are now always protected by your player ID. If the game can't connect, it tells you so; check your internet and try again.
