### Açıklama (1795/4000)

Akşam ne yesek? Hangi filmi izlesek? Hafta sonu nereye gitsek? Bitmeyen tartışmalara son!

Let's Picky, aile ve arkadaş gruplarının birlikte hızlıca karar vermesini sağlar. Bir oda kurun, 6 haneli kodu ya da davet bağlantısını paylaşın. Herkes kendi telefonunda kartları kaydırır. Herkesin beğendiği seçenek ortaya çıkınca konfetiler patlar ve karar verilmiş olur.

NASIL ÇALIŞIR?
• Oda kur: bir kategori seç, oda kodunu, QR kodunu ya da WhatsApp davetini paylaş.
• Kaydır: 60 saniye içinde kartları sağa (beğen) ya da sola (geç) kaydır. Her gün bir süper istek hakkın var.
• Eşleş: herkesin beğendiği kart ortak karar olur. Kimse anlaşamazsa "Kavanozdan Çek" herkese aynı kartı seçer.
• Git: sonuç ekranından haritada aç, fragmanı izle ya da sonucu paylaş.

90'dan fazla hazır kart, Türkçe ve İngilizce.

• Üyelik yok, profil yok, fotoğraf yok: yalnız geçici bir takma ad.

### Açıklama (1819/4000)

What should we eat tonight? Which movie should we watch? Where should we go this weekend? No more endless debates!

Let's Picky helps families and groups of friends decide together, fast. Create a room and share the 6-digit code or the invite link. Everyone swipes the cards on their own phone. When an option everyone likes appears, the confetti pops and the decision is made.

HOW IT WORKS
• Create a room: pick a category, then share the room code, the QR code or a WhatsApp invite.
• Swipe: in 60 seconds swipe right (like) or left (skip). You get one super like every day.
• Match: the card everyone likes becomes the shared decision. If nobody agrees, "Draw From the Jar" picks the same card for everyone.
• Go: open the result in maps, watch the trailer or share the result.

READY-MADE DECKS
• What to Eat? Lunch, dinner, dessert and coffee
• What to Watch? Family films, animation, documentaries, comedy, adventure
• Weekend Plans? Hiking, camping, museums, board games, the beach
• Quick Custom Decision: write your own options
• Special decks: Educational Games with Kids, Gourmet World Cuisine, Budget Student Menu
More than 90 ready-made cards, in Turkish and English.

SAFE AND FAMILY FRIENDLY
• No sign-up, no profiles, no photos: only a temporary nickname.
• No random matching or discovery: only people who know the code can join a room.
• No free-text chat: only a few fixed emoji reactions.
• Ads never interrupt a round: they appear only after the decision. Room data is deleted within 24 hours.

Play as a pair or with a big group: choose whether a decision needs two people or everyone to agree. Light and dark themes, Turkish and English. If a friend does not have the app, the invite link also opens in a web browser.

Next time someone asks "what should we do?", do not argue. Open Let's Picky!
