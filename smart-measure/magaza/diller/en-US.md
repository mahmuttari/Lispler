## Uzun açıklama

Measure Box is a multi-purpose toolkit that measures with your phone's sensors: a spirit level, a screen ruler, a protractor, distance and height by camera, a sound meter and a light meter in one app. Every tool says plainly how accurate it is, and the calibration centre lets you tune each one to your own phone.

📐 SPIRIT LEVEL
• Surface, line and vertical modes: level a shelf, a picture or a door frame.
• Degrees or percent grade; vibration and a beep when you reach the target (tolerance ±0.1° to ±2°).
• Two-step flip calibration and "set current angle as zero".

📷 MEASURE BY CAMERA
• Distance between two points on the floor, the height of an object and its width.
• Trigonometry from the camera direction and the height you hold the phone at; every result comes with its estimated uncertainty (±).
• Torch and full-screen view. The camera picture is only shown on the screen; it is not recorded or sent.

📏 SCREEN RULER AND PROTRACTOR
• Two guides in millimetres, centimetres or inches; a full-screen vertical ruler for longer lengths.
• Calibrate once with a bank card: the pixel density a phone reports can be off by 1–2 %.
• Protractor: inner and outer angle, 0.5° steps, lock the angle.

🔊 SOUND METER
• A-weighted sound level: live, average and maximum, with a graph of the last minute.
• Calibrate with a reference sound level meter to see dB(A), levels from whisper to concert, and hearing information for long exposure.
• The microphone is used only to work out the level; nothing is recorded.

💡 LIGHT METER
• Ambient light in lux, with average and maximum.
• Advice for a desk (300–500 lux recommended) and for indoor plants.

🕘 HISTORY AND FAVORITES
• Measurements you copy or finish are kept on this phone only; copy, share or clear them.
• Star the tools you use most and open them from the home screen.

🎨 Light and dark theme · English and Turkish · metric and imperial units

🔒 PRIVACY
• No account, no server of ours. Your history, favorites and calibrations stay on your phone.
• The camera picture and the microphone sound are processed on the phone only; they are never recorded, stored or sent. The app goes online only for ads and the Google Play subscription.

ℹ️ GOOD TO KNOW
• Phone sensors are not laboratory instruments. The level is typically good to ±0.2° to ±0.5°; camera measurement is typically within 3–10 % (a flat floor and a phone held still at the same height are assumed; no laser or depth sensor is used).
• Phone microphones are not measurement microphones: until you calibrate, the sound level is relative (dBFS) only. Do not use sound or light readings for legal or workplace-safety decisions.
• Light sensors can differ by roughly 20–30 % between phones.
• If your phone lacks a sensor (for example a light sensor), the tool that needs it says so; the other tools keep working.

The app shows ads: a banner at the bottom of the screen, and a full-screen ad only when you leave a tool, at most once every 4 minutes and 6 times an hour; never while you measure or while the camera or microphone is on. An optional ad-free subscription removes all ads and can be cancelled in Google Play at any time.
